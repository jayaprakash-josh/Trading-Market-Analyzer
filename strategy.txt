For every stock calculate:

------------------------------------------------------------

A +2% gap should not automatically score better than a +5% gap.

Suggested conceptual buckets:

+1% to +2%:

+2% to +4%:

> +5%:

------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------

    (latest_iep * latest_ieq) / 10,000,000

- executable value

------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------

mo_imbalance_ratio =
    market_order_imbalance_qty / max(ieq, 1)

47. IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE
============================================================

The system must answer:

"At 09:07 AM, which stocks have the strongest combination of persistent demand, executable liquidity, stable/increasing IEP, stable/increasing IEQ, positive equilibrium imbalance, positive market-order imbalance, and sufficient room for a profitable trade?"

It must NOT answer:

"Which stock has the highest IEP?"

The following hierarchy should guide the model:

1. PERSISTENT ORDER FLOW
2. EQUILIBRIUM IMBALANCE
3. IEQ / EXECUTABLE LIQUIDITY
4. IEP STABILITY / TREND
5. MARKET-ORDER CONFIRMATION
6. GAP QUALITY
7. STRETCH / CHASE RISK
8. MARKET CONTEXT
9. CATALYST

48. MOST IMPORTANT REAL-WORLD LESSON
============================================================

A displayed +5% IEP at one moment is NOT enough.

Example:

09:00 IEP = +5%
09:02 IEP = +5%
09:04 IEP = +5%
09:06 IEP = +3%
09:07 IEP = +1%

This stock must lose substantial points.

Another example:

09:00 IEP = +1%
09:02 IEP = +1.2%
09:04 IEP = +1.5%
09:06 IEP = +1.7%
09:07 IEP = +1.9%

with:

IEQ increasing
EQ imbalance increasing
MO imbalance positive
high liquidity
low reversal risk

This stock should score very well even though its final gap is much smaller.

============================================================
49. FINAL TRADING PHILOSOPHY
============================================================

The purpose is NOT to maximize the number of trades.

The purpose is to identify only situations where multiple independent signals align.

Therefore:

FEWER HIGH-QUALITY SIGNALS > MANY WEAK SIGNALS

If the data is ambiguous:

CAUTION or NO-GO

If the signal is contradictory:

NO-GO

If there is no strong candidate:

PRIMARY = NONE

Never force the model to select a stock.
