## 📈 Extra Trading Intelligence: How to Handle These

Tier 3 stocks are like riding a tiger. They will give you your fastest 20% gains, but they will eat you alive if you fall asleep.

### 1. The Breakout Characteristic (How to enter)
These stocks thrive on **narrative and momentum**. 
* Do not buy them when they are "boring" or consolidating for months. 
* You only enter when the AI script flags a **+8 or +9 Score** with massive delivery volume breakout. You want to ride the wave of institutional FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out).

### 2. Trailing Stop-Loss Strategy
Because you are strictly forbidden from averaging down on these, you must ruthlessly protect profits.
* If you buy `ZOMATO` at ₹250 and it goes to ₹275 (10% profit), you **immediately move your stop-loss to ₹250 (Breakeven).** 
* From that point on, it is a risk-free trade. If it drops back to ₹250, you get stopped out for zero loss. If it goes to ₹300, you trail the stop-loss to ₹275.

### 3. The Cash-Market Liquidity Trap (KAYNES, SAFARI, RVNL, IREDA)
Notice that almost half of these are **Cash Market Only (❌ F&O)**. 
* This means they have **Circuit Limits** (5%, 10%, or 20%). 
* If `IREDA` reports terrible earnings, it will open exactly at -10% (Lower Circuit). The exchange will freeze trading, and you will not be able to sell your shares. 
* **This is exactly why you never invest heavy capital here.** If a Tier 3 cash stock hits a lower circuit, you put in a market sell order every morning at 09:00 AM until it finally executes. Do not hold it hoping for a bounce.
