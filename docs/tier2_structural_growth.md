## 📈 Extra Trading Intelligence: How to Handle These

Tier 2 stocks are the "Multi-Baggers" of your portfolio. They provide the actual alpha (market-beating returns), but they are priced for perfection.

### 1. The "One-Tranche" Average Down Rule
Because these stocks have high P/E ratios (often 50 to 80), a market correction can easily shave 30% off their price just to bring the valuation back to "normal." 
* If you swing trade `KPITTECH` and it drops 10%, **check the news**. 
* If the drop is due to global Nasdaq weakness (no bad news for KPIT specifically), you add **exactly ONE tranche** at a major support level (like the 100-DMA).
* If it keeps dropping after that, **you stop buying.** You let it bottom out completely and wait for the next quarterly earnings report to confirm the growth story is intact.

### 2. The "Earnings Season" Danger Zone
Tier 2 stocks are hyper-sensitive to quarterly earnings. 
* If `PERSISTENT` or `VBL` reports a mere 12% profit growth when the market expected 18%, the stock will hit a 10% lower circuit instantly. 
* **Pro-Tip:** Never initiate a fresh, full-sized swing trade 3 days before a Tier 2 stock's earnings date. It is pure gambling.

### 3. Sector Rotation Awareness
Unlike Tier 1 (which always recovers fast), Tier 2 stocks are subject to multi-year sector rotations. 
* For example, Specialty Chemicals (`DEEPAKNTR`) were the darlings of 2021 but went into a brutal 2-year bear market due to Chinese dumping.
* **If the entire sector is in a downtrend, do not average down on the stock.** Wait for sector rotation to bring money back into the industry.
