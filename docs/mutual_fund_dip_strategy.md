# Mutual Fund Dip-Buying Strategy: The Complete Playbook

> **Your Goal:** Instead of blind SIPs, you want to tactically deploy lump sums during market corrections, hold for mid-term (1-3 years), and book profits when the market recovers.
>
> This is NOT conventional "buy and hold forever" advice. This is a quantitative, rules-based strategy optimized for your trading mindset.

---

## PART 1: The 3 Dip-Detection Triggers

You need objective, measurable signals to tell you WHEN to buy. Here are 3 battle-tested triggers used by smart money managers on Indian forums (Freefincal, IndiaInvestments Reddit, Subramoney):

### Trigger 1: India VIX Spike (Fear Gauge)
| Parameter | Rule |
|---|---|
| **What to watch** | India VIX (available on NSE website or your broker app) |
| **Normal range** | 10-15 |
| **Buy Signal (Small Tranche)** | VIX crosses above **18** → Deploy 25% of your MF war-chest |
| **Buy Signal (Heavy Tranche)** | VIX crosses above **25** → Deploy 50% of your MF war-chest |
| **Buy Signal (All-In)** | VIX crosses above **30** (rare, happens during wars/pandemics) → Deploy remaining 25% |
| **Why it works** | VIX measures fear. When VIX spikes, retail investors panic-sell their MF units. NAVs crash. You buy their fear at a discount. |

### Trigger 2: Nifty PE Ratio (Valuation Gauge)
| Parameter | Rule |
|---|---|
| **What to watch** | Nifty 50 PE Ratio (check on Trendlyne or NSE India website) |
| **Overvalued (DON'T buy)** | PE > 24 → Market is expensive. Do NOT deploy fresh capital. |
| **Fair Value (Start SIP)** | PE between 20-24 → Normal SIP is fine, no lump sum needed. |
| **Undervalued (BUY the dip)** | PE < 20 → Deploy a lump sum immediately. |
| **Deep Value (Generational buy)** | PE < 17 → This happens once every 5-8 years (COVID crash, 2008). Go all-in. |
| **Why it works** | PE tells you how many rupees you are paying for ₹1 of Nifty earnings. Below 20, you are statistically buying cheap. |

### Trigger 3: Drawdown from All-Time High (Technical Gauge)
| Parameter | Rule |
|---|---|
| **What to watch** | Nifty 50 closing price vs its All-Time High |
| **5% Drawdown** | Deploy 20% of war-chest into Mid Cap / Flexi Cap funds |
| **10% Drawdown** | Deploy another 30% into Mid Cap / Small Cap funds |
| **15%+ Drawdown** | Deploy remaining 50% aggressively into Small Cap funds |
| **Why it works** | Historical data shows that every 10%+ Nifty correction has recovered within 12-18 months. You are buying the recovery. |

> **PRO TIP:** The strongest buy signal is when ALL 3 triggers fire simultaneously (VIX > 25 + PE < 20 + Drawdown > 10%). This has happened only 4 times in the last 15 years: 2008, 2013, 2020 (COVID), and briefly in 2022. Every single time, the market was 40-80% higher within 2 years.

## PART 3: The Execution Framework

### Step 1: Build the War-Chest
Every month, instead of blindly doing a SIP into equity funds, transfer your monthly investment amount into the **Liquid/Money Market fund** (#7 or #8). Let it accumulate. You are earning 7% while waiting.

### Step 2: Wait for Triggers
Check India VIX and Nifty PE once a week (every Sunday evening). It takes 30 seconds.

### Step 3: Deploy on Dips
When any trigger fires:

| Trigger Fired | Action | Where to Deploy |
|---|---|---|
| VIX > 18 OR 5% Drawdown | Deploy 25% of war-chest | Flexi Cap funds (#1, #2) |
| VIX > 25 OR 10% Drawdown | Deploy another 30% of war-chest | Mid Cap funds (#3, #4) |
| VIX > 30 OR 15%+ Drawdown | Deploy remaining war-chest | Small Cap funds (#5, #6) |

### Step 4: Book Profits (Mid-Term Exit Strategy)
This is where most people fail. They buy the dip but never sell. Here is your profit-booking rule:

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Your MF investment is up **20%** | Redeem 25% of units → Move profits back to Liquid fund |
| Your MF investment is up **35%** | Redeem another 25% of units → Move profits back to Liquid fund |
| Your MF investment is up **50%+** | Redeem 50% of remaining units → You have now recovered your entire principal + profit |
| Nifty PE crosses above **24** | Redeem ALL remaining equity MF units regardless of profit %. Market is overheated. |

## PART 5: What NOT To Do

1. **Do NOT do SIP into Small Cap funds at all-time highs.** You are buying expensive units every month. Wait for a 10%+ correction and deploy a lump sum instead.
2. **Do NOT hold more than 3-4 equity MF funds.** Holding 10 funds gives you "diworsification" — your portfolio starts looking like the Nifty 500 index, but with higher expense ratios.
3. **Do NOT buy NFOs (New Fund Offers).** They have zero track record. AMCs launch NFOs during bull markets to collect maximum AUM from FOMO investors.
4. **Do NOT buy Regular plans.** Always buy Direct plans through Groww, Zerodha Coin, or Kuvera. Regular plans pay 1-1.5% annual commission to the distributor, which comes directly out of YOUR returns.
