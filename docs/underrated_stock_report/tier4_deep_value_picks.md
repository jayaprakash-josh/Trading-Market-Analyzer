# Tier 4: Deep Value Stock Picks — The Watchlist

> **Framework Used:** 4-Stage Multiplicative Model (Value × Quality × Catalyst), Hard-Stop Kill Switch, Risk-Reward Ranking.
> **Universe:** NSE-listed stocks with daily volume > ₹5 Cr.
> **Date:** September 2026.

---

## How to Read This Document

Each stock below has been stress-tested through our approved framework:
- **Value Score:** How cheap is it vs its own history? (Not vs other stocks)
- **Quality Score:** Is the underlying business still excellent?
- **Catalyst Score:** What will force the market to re-price it?
- **Hard-Stop Check:** Any fatal flaws? (Governance, pledge, structural decline)
- **Risk-Reward:** Upside vs Downside ratio.
- **Verdict:** GO / WATCH / TRACK / NO-GO
